Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026Last updated September 24, 2026

The Engine turns a musician's songs and footage into short-form videos and posts them to the social accounts the musician connects. This policy explains what information The Engine collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The Engine is operated by Max Totten ("we", "us"). When this policy says "you", it means the person using The Engine.

1. Information we collect

Account information. Your email address, which you use to sign in to the dashboard, and the settings you choose, such as posting schedules, captions and connected accounts.

Connected platform accounts. When you connect TikTok, YouTube or Instagram, the platform gives us an access token and a refresh token that let The Engine act on your behalf. We also receive basic profile details: your account or channel ID, display name and avatar. We store every token encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and we decrypt a token only at the moment it is needed to make a request to that platform.

Media you provide. The songs, audio, video clips and YouTube links you upload or submit, plus the videos, clips and captions The Engine creates from them.

Billing information. Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. From Stripe we receive your customer ID, subscription plan and status, and payment status.

Technical information. Server logs such as request times, error messages and IP addresses. We use them to run the service, secure it and fix problems.

2. How we use information

  • To create videos from your media and post them to the accounts you connect, at the times you schedule.
  • To keep you signed in and to send you service emails, such as sign-in links, alerts when a post fails, and billing notices.
  • To process your subscription payments.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use your media or platform data to train machine-learning models.

3. Platform-specific data use

YouTube. The Engine uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the channels you connect. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke The Engine's access at any time from your Google security settings.

The Engine's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TikTok. The Engine uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API to identify your account and to upload or publish the videos you schedule. We use TikTok data only to post your content and to show you the status of your posts.

Instagram. The Engine uses the Instagram Graph API to publish Reels to the professional accounts you connect. We use Instagram data only to post your content and to show you the status of your posts.

We request only the permissions needed to post on your behalf, and we never post anything you did not schedule or approve in your settings.

4. How we share information

We share information only in these cases:

  • With the platforms you connect, when we send them the videos and captions you schedule.
  • With service providers who help us run The Engine, such as hosting, video rendering, email delivery and payment processing (Stripe). They may use the information only to provide those services to us.
  • When the law requires it, or when we need to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or others.
  • In a business transfer. If The Engine is sold or merged, your information would pass to the new owner under the terms of this policy.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you disconnect a platform, we delete its tokens right away. If you delete your account, we delete your account information, tokens and stored media within 30 days. The exception is billing records, which we keep for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Videos that were already posted stay on the platforms until you remove them there.

How to delete your data. Disconnect a platform from the dashboard's Accounts page, or email maxtotten01@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my data". We will confirm when the deletion is complete.

You can also revoke access directly on each platform: in Google security settings, in the TikTok app under Settings › Security › Manage app permissions, or in Instagram's Settings › Apps and websites.

6. Security

We encrypt platform tokens at rest, use HTTPS for all traffic, and limit access to production systems. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to or limit certain uses of it. California residents have these rights under the CCPA, and people in the EEA and UK have them under the GDPR. To use any of these rights, email us. We will not treat you differently for doing so.

8. Children

The Engine is not intended for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age required by any platform you connect. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also notify you by email or in the dashboard before it takes effect.
