Your songs, cut into shorts and posted on schedule.

The Engine turns a musician's tracks and footage into short-form videos, then publishes them to the accounts the artist owns, day after day, without anyone editing by hand.

TikTokYouTube ShortsInstagram Reels
Bring the source

Songs and footage

Upload audio and clips, or point The Engine at a music video. It splits the footage into scenes and transcribes the lyrics.

Cut the videos

Edited to the beat

Each short is cut to the track's rhythm with on-screen lyric captions, in the artist's chosen visual style.

Post them

On the artist's accounts

Connect TikTok, YouTube and Instagram once. The Engine posts at the cadence you set, and alerts you if a post fails.

Built for artists and their teams

Account ownership
Videos go out on the artist's own channels. The Engine never posts from shared or borrowed accounts.
Per-artist style
Each artist gets their own visual style, caption treatment and posting schedule.
Access you control
Platform connections use each platform's official sign-in. The tokens they return are stored encrypted, and you can disconnect any account at any time.
Simple billing
A monthly subscription, billed through Stripe. Cancel whenever you like.
